Responsible Gaming Policy

At FT7.GAME, we are committed to promoting a fun, secure, and controlled environment for all online competitive gaming enthusiasts. We strongly believe that playing on FT7.COM should always remain an entertaining and engaging activity.

1. Principles of Responsible Gaming

Online skill-based gaming is meant for recreational purposes. To ensure a healthy relationship with the FT7 GAME app, players are encouraged to follow these fundamental practices:

  • Game for Entertainment: Treat competitive matches as a form of leisure and skill-building rather than a primary source of income.
  • Set Time Limits: Allocate specific time slots for gaming and ensure it does not interfere with daily obligations, work, or family time.
  • Play in Control: Never play under emotional stress, exhaustion, or peer pressure.

FT7.COM strictly prohibits underage individuals from creating accounts or participating in games. We implement strict age-verification procedures on the official FT7 GAME software to prevent minors from accessing adult gaming services.

We provide built-in account management tools to help players stay within healthy limits:

  • Account Self-Exclusion: Contact our support team on FT7.COM for long-term account exclusion options.

4. Recognizing Problem Gaming Signals

It is crucial to recognize warning signs early. You may need to evaluate your gaming habits if you experience any of the following:

  • Neglecting work, studies, or personal relationships to play games continuously.
  • Feeling irritable, anxious, or stressed when unable to access FT7 GAME.
  • Spending more time or energy on gaming than originally intended.

5. Support and Assistance
